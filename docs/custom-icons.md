# Custom Recipe Icon Illustration Guide

This document defines the visual specification, generation prompts, and asset directory conventions for custom recipe illustration images in **Noonarby Casa Recipes**.

---

## 🎨 Visual Style Guide

Custom recipe cards use hand-drawn illustration artwork that matches the Great British Baking Show style of the site's official recipe images (_Cozy Chickpea Curry_, _Grilled Cheese_, etc.):

- **Medium & Technique:** Hand-drawn watercolor and colored pencil illustration with fine dark ink contour lines (`#00518c`) and subtle cross-hatching.
- **Background:** Solid, clean off-white / cream paper canvas (`#fdfcf7` or `#f8f6f0`) without outer circles, geometric badges, or background frames.
- **Palette Accent:** Soft blue watercolor washes (`#00518c` tone variations) to match the site's Noonblue design system identity.
- **Canvas & Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 square aspect ratio (`1000x1000px` or `800x800px`), centered subject.

---

## 📝 Core Prompt Formula for Nano Banana

Include this base style prefix in all image generation prompts:

> **Base Style Prefix:**
> `"Hand-drawn watercolor and colored pencil food illustration, Great British Baking Show style, fine dark ink line art, soft blue watercolor washes with subtle warm highlights, centered subject isolated on a plain off-white cream paper background, high detail, clean sketch aesthetic, 1:1 aspect ratio --no circle border, frame, shadow"`

---
