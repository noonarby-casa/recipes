## Instructions

1. **Marinate the onion:** In a medium bowl, combine the chopped red onion ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}) and fresh lime juice ({{< qty "3 tablespoons" >}}). Let stand for {{< timer "10 minutes" >}}.
2. **Mix ingredients:** Add the cubed watermelon ({{< qty "4 cups" >}}), Kalamata olives ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}), parsley ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}), and mint ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}) to the bowl.
3. **Chill:** Cover and chill in the refrigerator for {{< timer "1 hour" >}}.
4. **Garnish & serve:** Sprinkle crumbled feta cheese ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}) over the top just before serving. Frequently served on a bed of greens.
