## Instructions

1. Melt the butter ({{< qty "3 tablespoons" >}}) in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion ({{< qty "1 large" >}}) and red potatoes ({{< qty "3 medium" >}}), and cook, stirring often, for {{< timer "5 minutes" >}}.

2. Add the all-purpose flour ({{< qty "3 tablespoons" >}}), and cook, stirring constantly, for {{< timer "1 minute" >}}.

3. Add the chicken broth ({{< qty "4 cups" >}}), and bring to a boil; reduce heat, and simmer, covered, for {{< timer "5 minutes" >}} or just until the potatoes are tender.

4. Stir in the corn ({{< qty "2 cups" >}}), heavy cream ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}), paprika ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}), and sea salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste; return to a simmer, stirring occasionally.

5. Add the salmon pieces ({{< qty "4 fillets" >}}), and simmer for {{< timer "4 minutes" >}} or just until the fish flakes.

6. Add fresh lemon juice (to taste) and chopped fresh dill ({{< qty "1 to 2 tablespoons" >}}) and fresh parsley ({{< qty "1 to 2 tablespoons" >}}); season with additional sea salt and pepper, if desired.

### Tips & Notes

- **Fish Variations:** Any firm-fleshed fish like cod, scrod, or halibut works great as a substitute for salmon.
- **Yield:** 4 servings (approx. 7 ½ cups).
