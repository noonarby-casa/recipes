## Instructions

1. **Toast the Baguette**: Slice {{< qty "1" >}} baguette into 1/2-inch rounds (about 16–20 slices). Arrange the slices in a single layer on a baking sheet or toaster oven rack and toast in a toaster oven for {{< timer "3-5 minutes" >}} until lightly golden and crisp.
2. **Make the Guacamole**: In a medium bowl, combine {{< qty "2" >}} pitted and peeled avocados, {{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}} fresh lemon juice, and {{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}} guacamole seasoning mix. Mash with a fork until well combined and desired consistency is reached.
3. **Assemble & Garnish**: Spread a generous spoonful of guacamole evenly over each toasted baguette slice. Sprinkle with {{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}} everything bagel seasoning and serve immediately.
