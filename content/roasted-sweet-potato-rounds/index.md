## Instructions

1. **Preheat and Prepare:** Preheat oven to 400°F. Lightly spray a baking sheet with cooking spray.

2. **Roast the Sweet Potatoes:** Brush the sweet potato rounds ({{< qty "3" >}}) on both sides with olive oil. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Arrange in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Roast on the middle rack for {{< timer "20-25 minutes" >}}, turning halfway through, until tender in the center and golden brown. Remove from the oven and set aside to cool slightly.

3. **Lower Oven Temp:** Turn the oven down to 350°F.

4. **Season the Seeds:** In a bowl, toss the pumpkin seeds ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}), lime juice ({{< qty "2 teaspoons" >}}), chili powder ({{< qty "1 teaspoon" >}}), salt ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), and cayenne pepper ({{< qty "1/8 teaspoon" >}}) until the seeds are evenly coated.

5. **Toast the Seeds:** Pour the pumpkin seeds onto a separate baking sheet. Bake in the oven for about {{< timer "10 minutes" >}} until toasted and fragrant.

6. **Mash the Goat Cheese:** While the seeds are toasting, add the room temperature herbed goat cheese ({{< qty "300 grams" >}}) to a bowl and mash and stir until soft and spreadable.

7. **Assemble and Serve:** Place the roasted sweet potato rounds on a serving platter. Top each round with a dollop of mashed goat cheese followed by a sprinkle of spiced pumpkin seeds. Serve warm or at room temperature.
