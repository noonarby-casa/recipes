## Instructions

1. **Sweet Potatoes:** Preheat the oven to 425°F. Peel and cut the sweet potatoes ({{< qty "2" >}}) into wedges. Place on a large baking sheet and toss with the avocado oil ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}) and taco seasoning ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}) until coated.
2. **Onions:** Peel the red onion ({{< qty "1" >}}) and cut it into thin wedges. Tuck them onto the baking sheet in and around the sweet potatoes.
3. **Roast:** Transfer the whole pan to the oven and bake for a total of {{< timer "35 minutes" >}}. Take them out at {{< timer "20 minutes" >}} to flip the pieces and scoot everything over to add the chicken meatballs ({{< qty "1 pound" >}}) so they get the last {{< timer "15 minutes" >}} of bake time.
4. **Sauce:** While the sweet potatoes are roasting, blend up the sauce ingredients — avocado oil ({{< qty "1/3 cup" >}}), half avocado ({{< qty "1/2" >}}), orange juice ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}), white vinegar ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), garlic ({{< qty "1 clove" >}}), cilantro ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}), kosher salt ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), and optional mayo ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}). Taste, adjust, and set aside.
5. **Serve:** Pour some mojo sauce into bowls or on plates. Pile high with roasted meatballs, sweet potato wedges, and onions. Enjoy!
