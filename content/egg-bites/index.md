## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and generously coat a 24-count mini muffin tin with nonstick cooking spray.
2. In a medium bowl, toss together the shredded cheddar ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}) and chopped bacon ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}). Divide the mixture evenly among the 24 mini muffin cups (about 1 rounded teaspoon each).
3. Crack the eggs ({{< qty "8 large" >}}) into a large measuring cup or bowl and whisk just until smooth. Add the milk ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}), garlic powder ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), onion powder ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), kosher salt ({{< qty "1 teaspoon" >}}), and black pepper ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}); whisk until thoroughly combined.
4. Divide the egg mixture evenly among the mini muffin cups (the cups will be about three-quarters full), then gently stir each one with a small spoon or butter knife.
5. Bake for {{< timer "14 to 18 minutes" >}}, until the egg bites are puffed and just set on top. Cool in the tin for at least {{< timer "10 minutes" >}}, then remove from the pan and serve.
6. To store, allow the egg bites to cool completely before transferring to an airtight container and refrigerating for up to 2 days or freezing for up to 3 months. To reheat from refrigerated, bake covered at 350°F for {{< timer "8 to 10 minutes" >}} or microwave for {{< timer "30 to 45 seconds" >}}.
