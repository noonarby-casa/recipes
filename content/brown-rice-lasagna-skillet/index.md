## Instructions

1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, add {{< qty "2 teaspoons" >}} of the olive oil. Add the Italian turkey sausage ({{< qty "1 pound" >}}), removed from the casings. Season with salt and black pepper, break up into small pieces with a spoon, and brown. Once browned, remove to a plate and set aside.

2. In the same pan, add the remaining {{< qty "2 teaspoons" >}} olive oil, diced yellow onion ({{< qty "1/2 large" >}}), and minced garlic ({{< qty "3 cloves" >}}). Season with salt and black pepper. Cook until the onions have softened, {{< timer "3-5 minutes" >}}.

3. Add the turkey sausage back into the skillet with the onions and garlic. Season with the dried oregano ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}) and Italian seasoning ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}). Stir together until combined.

4. Add the marinara sauce ({{< qty "2 1/2 cups" >}}). Bring to a boil, reduce the heat to a simmer, and simmer for {{< timer "10 minutes" >}}. Season to taste with salt and black pepper.

5. Stir in the cooked brown rice ({{< qty "2 cups" >}}) until fully combined (ensure the rice is well-seasoned with salt and pepper).

6. Finish the dish with cheese using one of two options:
   - **Stir-in:** Add the shredded mozzarella ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}) and ricotta ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}), stir together until melted and combined, and serve immediately.
   - **Broiled Top:** Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}) over the top and broil in the oven for {{< timer "2-3 minutes" >}} until melted and bubbly. Remove from the oven and top with dollops of ricotta ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}).

7. Garnish with sliced fresh basil over the top and serve warm.
