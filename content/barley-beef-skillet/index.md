## Instructions

1. In a large cast-iron or heavy skillet, cook the ground beef ({{< qty "1 pound" >}}) and chopped onion ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}) over medium heat until the meat is no longer pink.

2. Add the minced garlic ({{< qty "1 clove" >}}) and cook for {{< timer "1 minute" >}} longer. Drain any excess fat.

3. Add the beef broth ({{< qty "14 1/2 ounces" >}}), tomato sauce ({{< qty "8 ounces" >}}), and water ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}). Bring the mixture to a boil.

4. Stir in the chopped carrots ({{< qty "2 small" >}}), seeded and chopped tomato ({{< qty "1 small" >}}), chopped zucchini ({{< qty "1 small" >}}), pearl barley ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}), Italian seasoning ({{< qty "2 teaspoons" >}}), salt ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}), and black pepper ({{< qty "1/8 teaspoon" >}}).

5. Reduce heat to low, cover with a lid, and simmer until the barley is tender, {{< timer "20-25 minutes" >}}.
