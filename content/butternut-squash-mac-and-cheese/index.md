## Instructions

1. **Preheat**: Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. **Cook Macaroni**: Cook dry whole-wheat macaroni ({{< qty "1 pound" >}}) according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
3. **Sauté Veggies**: Heat olive oil ({{< qty "1 teaspoon" >}}) in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add chopped onion ({{< qty "1 medium" >}}) and red bell peppers ({{< qty "2 medium" >}}); cook, stirring frequently, for {{< timer "4-6 minutes" >}}, or until onion is translucent. Set aside.
4. **Simmer Squash**: Combine cubed butternut squash ({{< qty "3 cups" >}}), chicken broth ({{< qty "1 3/4 cups" >}}), and milk ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}) in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Reduce heat to medium; gently boil for {{< timer "22-25 minutes" >}}, or until squash is tender.
5. **Blend Sauce**: Place squash mixture in a blender or food processor; cover with lid and kitchen towel. Blend until smooth.
6. **Combine**: Combine squash mixture, macaroni, onion mixture, plain Greek yogurt ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}), shredded Gruyere cheese ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}), and shredded sharp cheddar cheese ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}). Season with sea salt and black pepper if desired; mix well.
7. **Bake**: Place macaroni mixture in a 13 x 9-inch ceramic or glass baking dish that has been coated with cooking spray. Bake for {{< timer "25-30 minutes" >}}, or until sauce is bubbling.
8. **Garnish & Serve**: Garnish with finely chopped fresh parsley if desired and serve.
