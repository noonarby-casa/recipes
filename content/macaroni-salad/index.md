## Instructions

1. Cook macaroni ({{< qty "8 ounces" >}} pasta) according to package directions and drain. Rinse thoroughly with cold water and drain again.
2. Mix mayonnaise ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}) with lemon juice ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), salt ({{< qty "1 teaspoon" >}}), and sugar ({{< qty "1 teaspoon" >}}).
3. Combine the dressing with the cooked macaroni, celery seed ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}), diced tomato ({{< qty "1 large" >}}), and chopped green pepper ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}).
4. Refrigerate before serving.
