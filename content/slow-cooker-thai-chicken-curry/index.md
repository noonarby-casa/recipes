## Instructions

1. Add the chicken stock ({{< qty "1-2 cups" >}}), Thai red curry paste ({{< qty "2-4 tablespoons" >}}), soy sauce ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), brown sugar ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), minced ginger ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), fish sauce ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), minced garlic ({{< qty "3 cloves" >}}), chicken thighs ({{< qty "1 pound" >}}), cubed kabocha squash ({{< qty "1 large" >}}), chopped yellow onion ({{< qty "1 medium" >}}), and sliced chili peppers ({{< qty "1-2" >}}) to your slow cooker and stir well until combined.
2. Cover and cook on High for {{< timer "4 hours" >}}.
3. After 4 hours, stir in the canned coconut milk ({{< qty "14 ounces" >}}) and torn kale ({{< qty "1 bunch" >}}). Let sit for a few minutes while you prepare jasmine rice, cauliflower rice, or quinoa for serving.
4. Ladle into bowls and top with fresh cilantro, lime wedges, and extra chopped chili peppers if desired. Serve warm!
