## Instructions

1. In a large Dutch oven or heavy-duty soup pot, heat the olive oil ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}) over medium heat. Add the mild Italian sausage ({{< qty "12 ounces" >}}) and chopped bacon ({{< qty "3 slices" >}}). Cook until sausage is browned and bacon is crisp, breaking up the meat as you go, {{< timer "8-10 minutes" >}}. Remove to a plate with a slotted spoon. Drain all but {{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}} of grease.

2. Add the diced onion ({{< qty "1 medium" >}}) and cook until soft, {{< timer "3-4 minutes" >}}. Add the minced garlic ({{< qty "2 cloves" >}}) and cook for {{< timer "30 seconds" >}}, stirring constantly.

3. Add the cauliflower florets ({{< qty "6 cups" >}}) and chicken broth ({{< qty "32 ounces" >}}). Season to taste with kosher salt and black pepper. Simmer, uncovered, until cauliflower is tender, {{< timer "15 minutes" >}}.

4. Puree the soup until smooth in either a high-powered blender or with an immersion blender.

5. Add the half and half ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}) and bring to a gentle simmer. Stir in the cooked sausage, bacon, and chopped kale ({{< qty "4 cups" >}}). Simmer until kale is wilted, {{< timer "5 minutes" >}}. Taste the soup for salt. Serve warm with a sprinkle of red pepper flakes, if desired.
