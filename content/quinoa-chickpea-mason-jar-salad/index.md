## Instructions

1. **Soak the Red Onion**: Dice {{< qty "1/2" >}} red onion and place it in a small bowl of cold water. Let it soak for {{< timer "5 minutes" >}} to take off the raw bite, then drain thoroughly.
2. **Make the Dressing**: In a small bowl or liquid measuring cup, whisk together {{< qty "2 tbsp" >}} fresh lemon juice, {{< qty "3 tbsp" >}} extra-virgin olive oil, {{< qty "1/2 tsp" >}} honey, {{< qty "1/2 tsp" >}} kosher salt, and {{< qty "1/4 tsp" >}} black pepper. Evenly divide the dressing among the bottoms of 3 large mason jars.
3. **Layer the Salad**: Divide and layer the ingredients evenly into the 3 jars in the following order:
   - **First Layer**: {{< qty "1 1/2 cups" >}} cooked and cooled quinoa
   - **Second Layer**: {{< qty "1 can" >}} (15-oz) drained and rinsed chickpeas
   - **Third Layer**: {{< qty "3" >}} diced mini cucumbers and the drained red onion
   - **Fourth Layer**: {{< qty "1/3 cup" >}} chopped fresh mint and {{< qty "1/4 cup" >}} chopped fresh parsley
   - **Top Layer**: {{< qty "1/3 cup" >}} roughly chopped toasted pistachios and {{< qty "1/3 cup" >}} crumbled feta cheese
4. **Store & Serve**: Seal the mason jars tightly and refrigerate for up to 4 days. If adding optional {{< qty "1" >}} diced avocado, add it right before serving. Give the jar a vigorous shake before eating straight from the jar or pouring into a bowl!
