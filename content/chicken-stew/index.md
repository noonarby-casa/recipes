## Instructions

1. Heat a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add butter ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}), olive oil ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}), diced onion ({{< qty "1 medium" >}}), diced carrots ({{< qty "2 medium" >}}), diced celery ({{< qty "3 stalks" >}}), minced garlic ({{< qty "4 cloves" >}}), sweet paprika ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), and a big pinch of salt. Cook, stirring frequently, until the onion is translucent, {{< timer "5 to 7 minutes" >}}.
2. Add all-purpose flour ({{< qty "3 tablespoons" >}}) and stir for {{< timer "1 minute" >}}. Add chicken broth ({{< qty "4 cups" >}}) and apple cider vinegar ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}) and stir until the flour is fully incorporated.
3. Add boneless skinless chicken thighs ({{< qty "1 1/2 pounds" >}}), baby potatoes ({{< qty "1 pound" >}}), green beans ({{< qty "1 cup" >}}), heavy cream ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}), dried sage ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), dried thyme ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), dried oregano ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), and {{< qty "1 teaspoon" >}} each of salt and black pepper. Bring the stew to a gentle boil on medium-high, then reduce heat to maintain a simmer. Simmer, with the lid partially covering the pot, until the chicken is cooked through, about {{< timer "20 minutes" >}}.
4. Transfer the chicken to a plate. Completely cover the pot with the lid and let the vegetables cook for {{< timer "5 to 10 minutes" >}}, until desired doneness. Meanwhile, shred the chicken into bite-size pieces using two forks, then return the chicken to the pot. Season to taste with additional salt and black pepper. Serve warm, garnished with fresh parsley and lemon wedges on the side, if desired.
