## Instructions

1. **Sauté the Aromatics:** In a large soup pot, melt the butter ({{< qty "4 tablespoons" >}}) over medium heat. Add the chopped carrots ({{< qty "3" >}}), sliced white onions ({{< qty "2" >}}), and grated garlic ({{< qty "8 cloves" >}}). Sauté for about {{< timer "20 minutes" >}} until soft and caramelized.
2. **Make the Base:** Sprinkle the all-purpose flour ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}) over the cooked vegetables and stir well so everything is evenly coated. Slowly pour in both the beef stock ({{< qty "3 1/2 cups" >}}) and chicken stock ({{< qty "3 cups" >}}) while stirring continuously to prevent any lumps.
3. **Season & Add Meatballs:** Stir in the fennel seeds ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), Worcestershire sauce ({{< qty "2 teaspoons" >}}), honey ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), sugar ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), soy sauce ({{< qty "1 1/2 tablespoons" >}}), and salt and black pepper to taste. Add the frozen meatballs ({{< qty "2 pounds" >}}) directly into the simmering pot. Let the soup bubble gently on medium-low heat for {{< timer "15-20 minutes" >}} until the meatballs are heated through and the vegetables are tender.
4. **Add Pasta & Cream:** Stir in the heavy cream ({{< qty "2 cups" >}}), sour cream ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}), lemon juice ({{< qty "1 small" >}} lemon), and fresh egg pasta ({{< qty "9 ounces" >}}). Simmer for another {{< timer "3-5 minutes" >}} until the pasta is cooked al dente and the broth is rich and creamy.
5. **Serve:** Stir in the chopped fresh parsley ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}) and adjust seasoning if necessary. Ladle into bowls and serve with a spoonful of lingonberry jam (or cranberry sauce) and an extra garnish of fresh parsley.
