## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Eggplant**: Preheat the oven to 350°F. Cut the eggplant ({{< qty "1" >}} medium to large) in half lengthwise and scoop out the eggplant flesh, leaving about 1/2-inch of flesh on the skin. Chop the scooped-out flesh coarsely and set it aside. Brush the inside of the eggplant shells with a little olive oil (from {{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}) and bake for {{< timer "30 minutes" >}}, then set aside.
2. **Sauté the Eggplant Filling**: Add half of the remaining olive oil to a large skillet. Sauté the chopped onion ({{< qty "1 large" >}}) and half of the minced garlic (from {{< qty "3 cloves" >}}) for {{< timer "3 minutes" >}}. Mix the chopped eggplant flesh into the onions and add the cinnamon ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}), paprika ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}), cayenne ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}), black pepper ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}), and curry powder ({{< qty "1 1/2 teaspoons" >}}). Sauté until lightly brown (the mixture should be slightly dry), then set aside.
3. **Cook the Beef and Sauce**: Add the remaining olive oil to the skillet and sauté the remaining garlic for a few minutes until aromatic. Add the ground beef ({{< qty "1/2 pound" >}}) and cook until browned, about {{< timer "5 minutes" >}}. Add the tomato paste ({{< qty "3-4 teaspoons" >}}), chopped tomatoes ({{< qty "1 1/2 cups" >}}), Kalamata olives ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}), lemon juice ({{< qty "1-2 teaspoons" >}}), parsley ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}), red wine ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}), and sugar ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}). Mix everything together and cook for another {{< timer "5 minutes" >}}.
4. **Assemble and Bake**: Combine the cooked eggplant mixture with the beef sauce mixture and spoon it into the baked eggplant shells. Top with goat cheese crumbles ({{< qty "1/4 cup" >}}) and bake for {{< timer "20 minutes" >}}.
