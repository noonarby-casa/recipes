## Instructions

### Bake the Crust

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl, stir together the graham-cracker crumbs ({{< qty "1 3/4 cups" >}}), melted butter ({{< qty "1 stick" >}}), and ground cinnamon ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}).
3. Press the mixture evenly into a 9-inch pie plate.
4. Bake for about {{< timer "12 minutes" >}} until firm.
5. Remove crust and lower oven temperature to 325°F.

### Make the Filling

6. In a large bowl, whisk egg yolks ({{< qty "3 large" >}}) with cream of tartar ({{< qty "1/4 teaspoon" >}}) until frothy.
7. Stir in the sweetened condensed milk ({{< qty "2 cans" >}} - 14 ounces each).
8. Whisk in the Key lime juice ({{< qty "2/3 cup" >}}) until fully combined.

### Bake & Chill

9. Pour filling into the warm crust and bake at 325°F for about {{< timer "15 minutes" >}}, or until set.
10. Cool completely on a wire rack, then refrigerate until thoroughly chilled before serving.

### Tips & Notes

- **Make Ahead:** Can be refrigerated overnight.
