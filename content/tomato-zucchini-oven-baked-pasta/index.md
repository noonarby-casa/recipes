## Instructions

1. **Preheat & Prep**: Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a deep 8-inch baking dish or 2-quart oven-safe casserole.
2. **Cook Pasta**: Cook the penne pasta ({{< qty "8 ounces" >}}) in boiling salted water according to the package directions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
3. **Sauté Zucchini**: While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}) in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the minced garlic ({{< qty "3 cloves" >}}) and zucchini ({{< qty "1" >}}). Stir well and cook until tender and golden, about {{< timer "10 minutes" >}}.
4. **Mix**: In the baking dish (or skillet if oven-safe), combine the pasta, sautéed zucchini, and spaghetti sauce ({{< qty "3 cups" >}}). Stir in the salt ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), black pepper ({{< qty "1/2 teaspoon" >}}), and chopped fresh parsley ({{< qty "2 tablespoons" >}}). Mix well until thoroughly combined.
5. **Bake**: Sprinkle the grated mozzarella ({{< qty "6 ounces" >}}) evenly over the top. Cover with foil and bake for {{< timer "20 minutes" >}}. Remove the foil and bake uncovered for a further {{< timer "10 minutes" >}} until the cheese is melted, golden, and bubbly.
6. **Serve**: Remove from the oven, let rest for a few minutes, and serve warm.
