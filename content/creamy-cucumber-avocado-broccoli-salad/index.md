## Instructions

1. Thinly slice {{< qty "4-5" >}} Persian cucumbers (or {{< qty "3" >}} Japanese cucumbers) into half moons, coins, or sticks. Go thin so they soak up all the dressing.
2. Add the cucumbers to a large bowl, mason jar, or deli container.
3. Add {{< qty "8 oz" >}} of finely chopped broccoli. Chop it small so you get a little in every bite.
4. Add {{< qty "1" >}} cubed avocado, a large handful ({{< qty "1/2 cup" >}}) of chopped mint, and {{< qty "1/4 cup" >}} of chopped chives (or thinly chopped green onions/scallions).
5. Toss in {{< qty "2 tbsp" >}} of roasted walnuts (or pistachios) for crunch.
6. Make the creamy garlic lime dressing: mix {{< qty "2 tbsp" >}} mayonnaise, {{< qty "2 tbsp" >}} Greek yogurt, {{< qty "1 tbsp" >}} Dijon mustard, zest of {{< qty "1" >}} lime, juice of {{< qty "2" >}} limes, {{< qty "1-2" >}} minced garlic cloves, and a generous pinch of salt. Pour it over the veggies.
7. Shake it all up or gently toss until coated.
8. Top with feta, more herbs, extra nuts, or a dusting of sumac if you’re feeling fancy.
