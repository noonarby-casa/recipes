## Instructions

1. **Brown the Beef & Season**: Heat a large pot over medium heat. Add the ground beef ({{< qty "1 to 1 1/2 pounds" >}}), season with the chili powder ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), paprika ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), cumin ({{< qty "1 tablespoon" >}}), and cayenne ({{< qty "1 teaspoon" >}}). Cook until browned, breaking up the meat with a wooden spoon, about {{< timer "6-8 minutes" >}}.
2. **Sauté Vegetables**: Add the diced yellow onion ({{< qty "1" >}} yellow onion), green bell pepper ({{< qty "1" >}} green bell pepper), and red bell pepper ({{< qty "1" >}} red bell pepper) to the pot. Cook until softened, about {{< timer "5 minutes" >}}.
3. **Add Beans & Tomatoes**: Add the drained and rinsed chickpeas ({{< qty "1 can" >}}), black beans ({{< qty "1 can" >}}), kidney beans ({{< qty "1 can" >}}), and crushed tomatoes ({{< qty "1 can" >}}) to the pot.
4. **Simmer**: Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for at least {{< timer "15-20 minutes" >}} (or longer if you prefer to let the flavors meld).
5. **Serve**: Serve in bowls topped with shredded Mexican cheese and Greek yogurt. Add optional tortilla chips or cornbread on the side.
